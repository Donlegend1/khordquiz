3-Note Stepwise

You will hear three notes that move up or down to the next note in the scale.

Listen to the phrase

Press Play. Listen for whether each note moves up or down. You can listen again whenever you need to.

Build your answer

Tap the note names on the keyboard in the order you hear them. Each tap adds the next note to your answer. Your answer needs three notes.

Check and submit

Read your notes from left to right. Use Undo to remove your last choice, or Clear to start your answer again. When you are ready, press Submit Answer.

What changes in this quiz?

The notes move between neighbouring names, such as Do to Re or Mi to Re. Tap one key for each note, in the order you hear it.

What changes in this quiz?

A skip is a jump past a note in the scale. For example, Do to Mi skips over Re. Listen to the whole phrase and enter all three notes in order.

Listen to the phrase

Build your answer

Tap the name of the higher note from each pair, in order. Your answer needs five notes.

What changes in this quiz?

Some notes sit between the seven white-key notes. Use the black keys for these sounds: Di, Ri, Fi, Si and Li. For example, Di sits between Do and Re. Enter every note you hear, including these connecting notes.
