Example answer

Lydian

This visual example shows Lydian over F bass. Choose Lydian. In your quiz, listen to the whole voicing and select the full name that matches.

What changes in this quiz?

A voicing is a way of arranging notes to make a chord sound. Here, each modal name has its own set of notes above a bass note. Listen to how those notes sound together. This lesson keeps F as the bass throughout. In the answer names, ♭ means lowered, ♯ means raised and ♮ means natural. A change of one keyboard step counts both white and black keys. The number beside a symbol describes a note’s place in the mode, counted from the bass note; it does not mean its position in the displayed chord. Choose the modal name rather than an inversion or a note name.

A voicing is a way of arranging notes to make a chord sound. Here, each modal name has its own set of notes above a bass note. Listen to how those notes sound together. This lesson uses all twelve bass notes. The same modal sound moves to different starting notes. In the answer names, ♭ means lowered, ♯ means raised and ♮ means natural. A change of one keyboard step counts both white and black keys. The number beside a symbol describes a note’s place in the mode, counted from the bass note; it does not mean its position in the displayed chord.
